# Sources and rights / 来源与权利

蘇清的音乐专辑 / SuQing's Music Albums adds playlist, inventory exchange,
device UI and single-player playback bridges. The stable Mod ID remains
`SuQingMusicCollections`.

本项目不包含、下载、重编码或重新分发第三方歌曲音频，也不收录 True Music
或音乐包的完整原始脚本、模型和贴图。运行时以原文件路径引用玩家自行安装的音源，
世界模型也继续引用 True Music。自制的磁带、黑胶图标和发布美术单独记录来源。
歌曲、艺人名称及必要的物品和音源映射用于识别兼容对象，不表示本项目拥有歌曲权利。

No original music recordings, complete upstream scripts, models or textures are
redistributed. Private 2D aliases reference the user's separately installed music
files and retain their original gain. Artist/song names and compatibility IDs
identify supported content; no rights to those recordings or upstream assets are
claimed. This project grants no blanket license over third-party material.

## Required base / 必需基础

- True Music B42, Workshop `3397198968`, Mod ID `truemusic`.

## Supported music packs / 已适配音乐包

Install and enable the packs whose songs you want. Missing packs do not provide
their tracks or eligible premade selections. This is a reviewed catalog, not an
automatic importer for every True Music addon.

- GIRLS BAND CRY Music — Workshop `3554671997`.
- Poppin'Party music b42 — Workshop `3585009083`.
- Anime music b42 (MyGO!!!!! / Ave Mujica / sumimi) — Workshop `3585014312`.
- Roselia Music — Workshop `3654879576`.
- The True Music base main-theme cassette and vinyl are also recognized.

原曲及上游模组权利归各自权利人。测试读取本机已安装文件不构成再次分发授权。
公开包只应包含本项目运行文件和经核对的自制美术；不要把本机 Workshop 音乐目录、
测试夹具、游戏 JAR、审计中的源文件副本或完整第三方素材放进上传 Contents。

## Known upstream issue / 已知上游问题

`PPP75` (新しい季節に) currently names a missing `PPP100.ogg` in its upstream
sound definition. It remains identified but unavailable in the reviewed catalog;
no alternate recording is guessed. Default artist selections exclude it. Existing
original items and the source records of old collected albums are not deleted.
